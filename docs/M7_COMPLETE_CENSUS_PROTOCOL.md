Each emitted probability is joined to the exact target placement's observed operation outcome. Each absent probability retains its original status and its observed outcome. No absent probability or unmeasured PMX calculation is replaced with zero. PMX was not part of frozen M7; a separate 480-row inventory states that scope and identifies the four retained raw development campaigns.

Signed error and absolute error are expressed in percentage points. Tables report mean forecast, mean observation, signed error, MAE, median/p90 absolute error, Brier, requests, available/expected cells and complete campaign counts. Condition tables retain all eight application conditions even if coverage is zero. Operation-level pooled estimates give each represented placement/fault condition equal weight; availability and error describe only issued forecasts. Quantiles are over issued campaign-operation errors. Observation over all cells is also shown to reveal selection.

Descriptive 95% percentile intervals use 10,000 campaign resamples within placement/fault conditions, seed 771001 deterministically keyed by group. An operation's row has one value per independent campaign. Paired operation comparisons resample the same campaign difference. Application-level paired contrasts require all three operations of both methods in a campaign, average those operations equally, then weight represented conditions equally; counts of all common operation cells and excluded partial campaigns remain explicit. Such a conditional contrast cannot describe the excluded cells. These intervals do not replace the frozen historical M7 t-based contrast and are not the new three-application confirmatory analysis.

All available rows are published. There is no global ranking over unequal coverage. Frozen stable-block-sensitivity rows are used to reproduce historical summaries; the new main tables show all_sequence and stable, since the 46-second sensitivity changes block compatibility only.
