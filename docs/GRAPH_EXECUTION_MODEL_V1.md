# Observed mandatory-call execution model v1

This is a separately versioned extension of [the static demand core](GRAPH_DEMAND_MODEL_V1.md), motivated by the scoped [H-EXEC result](milestones/H_EXEC_01_SEALED_STUDY_CONFIRMATION.md). The implemented state class changes: completion coordinates describe mandatory call groups in an attempted operation, rather than treating a transport probe as a static physical capability. This document and its implementation are frozen before the application qualification. No additional causal mechanism, primary method selection or main-series admission is claimed here.

## State, graph and observation law

The model is M = <G,R,P,Φ>. G contains operation-specific native cross-service parent relations and native DB CLIENT relations with explicit peer identity completion. R binds the declared target replicas to ordinary transport-eligibility coordinates; other nodes' eligibility is fixed true as an explicit scope assumption. Required nodes, mandatory call groups, optional groups, external roots and deadlines are source/contract declarations. An observed call alone does not establish mandatory status. Every required call group is bound to a particular discovered edge; no edge template is substituted for extraction.

A finite state comprises X (observed last-check transport eligibility), D (the joint footprint of replicas selected by mandatory target calls), C0 (entry protocol completion), Cg (completion of each mandatory call group), and T (whole-operation completion within 2 seconds). Values of C refer to the observed execution, not to how an unselected replica would have responded. Dependencies among all coordinates are unrestricted. Repeated calls contribute to a declared group, with all mandatory calls required to complete; ignored errors must have a source-specific exclusion. Rescued failed mandatory calls, unclassified retries, unseen execution branches and unmodelled payload corruption are outside this class.

For a complete state, let B be synchronous graph reachability of every declared required node under R/X. Let Rsel = B AND (for each controlled service, at least one replica is demanded and every demanded replica has all its eligibility gates true). Let C = C0 AND every Cg AND presence of its required synchronous edge in G. The execution predicate is E = Rsel AND C AND T. An absent structural requirement makes E false, including when another graph path reaches the same target. Optional synchronous work is excluded from C when its failure does not prevent the declared response, while its elapsed cost remains in the observed whole-operation T. Async work is not made a requirement for immediate completion.

The implemented ablations are B; Rsel; Rsel*C (without deadline); E; B*C*T (without selection); Rsel*T (without completion). They share one joint empirical observation law. None is obtained by multiplying independent marginal estimates or a fitted residual q. B is request-weighted here, unlike the earlier uniform-probe technical examples. It is not the unchanged published G0 algorithm.

Each external attempt contributes exactly one masked row, including failed and untraced attempts. For category o=(mask,values), let Q(o) be its observed frequency and F(o) its compatible Boolean states. The completion of masked coordinates is arbitrary and may depend on both state and mask; no MCAR or iid routing assumption is imposed. The estimator identifies this empirical category law, not physical component-cause probabilities or a population distribution from a finite sample.

## S3: semantic restriction and its exact boundary

Assume the above definitions. Pointwise, E <= Rsel <= B and E is no greater than each implemented ablation. Consequently E_P[B]-E_P[E] = P(B=1,E=0), for every state law P. Equality holds exactly when P(B=1,E=0)=0. Equality for every law supported on a set S holds exactly when B=E on S: sufficiency follows by integration, and necessity follows by putting unit mass on a state where they differ. These are elementary indicator/probability facts, not a claim of a new general theorem.

The connection to measured business availability additionally requires Y=E almost surely in the declared workload/environment class: source propagation and payload/fixture assumptions must hold, probe eligibility must be a necessary condition for this execution despite possible lag, call-group observation must match its declared completeness rule, and client timing must reflect the full contract boundary. These conditions are not inferred from an error-free span. If stale probes report DOWN on a successful call, or an HTTP 200 contains corrupt required content, Y can differ from E. Therefore the sign of B-Y is not constrained by S3. The main applied evaluation must test this adequacy, rather than report the identity as an empirical bias explanation.

Counterexamples: both L4 checks can be UP while a required call fails; all mandatory calls may complete after the deadline; a surviving replica may coexist with selection of a failing replica; optional email failure need not prevent checkout. The bounded controls encode each distinction. H-EXEC establishes one particular intervention effect, not the truth of every observation assumption on every application.

## I5: sharp masked-function bounds and exact calculation

For any implemented functional f, its identified range conditional on empirical Q is

L(f) = sum_o Q(o) min_{s in F(o)} f(s), U(f) = sum_o Q(o) max_{s in F(o)} f(s).

Proof: every compatible conditional law on F(o) has expectation between its minimum and maximum. Summing gives the bounds. Choosing a conditional point mass at a minimizing/maximizing state in each category attains each bound, so they are sharp. The target is point identified exactly when f is constant on each positive-frequency fiber. Different f extrema need not use the same completed law; a paired gap is evaluated as one functional on a common state. This is the finite version of the preceding masked-law identification result and a standard partial-identification construction, not a new general result.

The solver enumerates the masked X/D/T control bits (at most 10 declared control coordinates). With these fixed, every reported functional, including each paired ablation gap, is affine in the single Boolean conjunction C. The minimum and maximum achievable C are attained by replacing every masked completion bit with false/true. Thus at most two completion candidates per control assignment suffice, even when there are many mandatory groups. The resulting candidate states are actual fiber members. Missing required edges fix C=false. This proves that the reduced candidate search has the same extrema as full enumeration. Exact Fraction weights are used; no Monte Carlo is performed.

Controls compare all 3^7 observation fibers with full 2^7 enumeration of a small model, and check known outcomes, informative masking, ambiguity witnesses, shared-law gaps, graph mutation/equivalent edge order and replay in a fresh process. This establishes arithmetic within the class, not identification of physical capabilities, confidence-interval coverage or business adequacy.

For forecasting a later period, invariance of the relevant state/observation law is an additional assumption. Unknown target placement laws are not identified by source calibration alone. Transfer is therefore explicitly unsupported until separate identifying assumptions and a corresponding implemented transformation exist. Bounds here are not confidence intervals; finite-sample and between-campaign uncertainty remain separate analysis tasks.
