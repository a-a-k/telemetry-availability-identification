# V3 validation literature: source and scope addendum

10 September 2026. This editorial addition leaves the frozen experimental design unchanged.

D. Foures, V. Albert and A. Nketsa, *A new specification-based qualitative metric for simulation model validity*, Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory 66 (2016), 1–15, DOI 10.1016/j.simpat.2016.03.002. The publisher's abstract, introduction and section excerpts describe composition with an experimental frame and qualitative scenario assessment. Access here supports this methodological comparison, without a claim to have checked every proof. [Publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1569190X16000411).

D. A. Hollmann, M. Cristiá and C. Frydman, *A family of simulation criteria to guide DEVS models validation rigorously, systematically and semi-automatically*, Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory 49 (2014), 1–26, DOI 10.1016/j.simpat.2014.07.003. Full text accessed; sections 1–3 distinguish model/specification comparison, partition criteria and the assumed uniformity of configurations. Our article cites that scope. [Full text](https://sce.carleton.ca/faculty/wainer/papers/1-s2.0-S1569190X14001142-main.pdf).

The earlier Friederich/Lazarova-Molnar 2025 article, DOI 10.1177/00375497241302866, appears in SIMULATION 101(8), 863–888. The manuscript's former SIMPAT label was incorrect and has been corrected. [Publisher](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00375497241302866), [author institution](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/publications/data-driven-reliability-assessment-of-manufacturing-systems-using/).

The added references support specific attribution and conceptual distinctions. They are not comparator results, additional experimental requirements or a venue-acceptance claim.
