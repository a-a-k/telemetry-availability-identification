# V3 campaign analysis component v1

This component specifies the six primary MAE comparisons and missingness rules before any v3 main outcomes. It is not main admission, final method selection or a complete main analysis workflow. Main campaigns remain0. It accepts evaluator aggregates and frozen forecast statuses after the learner/PMX seals; production validation of those seals and role-isolated loading is still required outside this pure component. Transfer, stable views and full stage-cost reporting remain separate work.

## Full census and event

Production design: three applications, colocated/split, N/NC/ND/NCD, repetitions0–9; all ten declared operations. Require every planned campaign identity, every operation and every method status. Duplicate/missing identities, invalid denominators, NaN/nonfinite/out-of-range probabilities, missing reasons and stable-view substitution are rejected. Forecast status is ok, unsupported, failed or missing. Absence requires null probability and a reason; valid probability0 is a real forecast. Evaluator-unavailable/unqualified campaigns remain in the census with their reasons and receive no invented scores. Method versions and the final Gstar mapping must be bound before main dispatch; these symbolic names do not select a model.

For each qualified external operation, observed success is S/N over all issued test attempts. Signed error and absolute error are in percentage points. Brier is the exact mean of N binary-outcome scores, computed from S,N and a constant forecast. Per-operation means give equal weight to retained placement/failure conditions, then equal weight to campaigns within a condition. Overall own-support means give equal weight to available operations within each retained condition and equal weight to retained conditions. Own support is listed for every condition/operation, including zeros. Own-support MAEs over different populations do not establish a ranking. Median and p90 are unweighted quantiles over available campaign-operation absolute errors, explicitly distinct from standardized means.

## Primary common support and weights

For each application and each Gstar−G0/Gstar−PMX contrast, retain complete common campaigns: evaluator qualified and both forecasts finite for every operation of that application. Average paired absolute-error differences equally over the operations inside each campaign. Within each placement/failure condition average retained campaign differences equally, then average retained conditions equally. Sign<0 favors Gstar. This is a conditional comparison over the reported complete common support, not an estimate for absent operations or conditions.

Retain all partially common operation cells in a separate explicit census, alongside the complete-campaign primary subset. Thus a missing forecast for one operation does not erase its siblings from the publication record, but its campaign cannot contribute a partial operation mix to the primary estimate. List every planned condition, retained campaign count/IDs and all empty conditions. An empty condition contributes no invented zero and changes the reported scope; it is never silently renormalized as if full coverage remained. No complete common campaigns anywhere yields not estimable and null point/interval. Low error is not a support or qualification criterion.

## Conditional paired campaign bootstrap

Six planned contrasts remain the family even when one is not estimable. Alpha=.05/6; central confidence level99.1666666667%;10000 resamples. Within each retained condition resample its n complete common campaigns with replacement, sample size n. A campaign's operations and the two methods always move together. Then repeat the identical equal-condition mean calculation. This conditions on observed common support; it does not propagate uncertainty about future coverage or missingness. A retained condition with only one independent common campaign gets a point estimate and no bootstrap interval, marked insufficient independent common campaigns, rather than a degenerate precision claim.

The interval below/above zero is reported without a finite-sample family-coverage guarantee; nonsignificance is not equivalence. Coverage scope must appear next to every interval. No request-level resampling or outcome-dependent changes to N/seed/weights are allowed.
