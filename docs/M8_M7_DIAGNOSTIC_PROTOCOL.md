# M8: post-M7 diagnostic protocol

## Status and purpose

M8 is a post-result diagnostic study. It does not amend the frozen M7
predictions, inclusion rules, or confirmatory analysis. Its purpose is to find
which explanations of the observed incompleteness and prediction discrepancy
are supported by preserved evidence before any method or parser is changed.

The current scientific position is deliberately non-terminal: M7 established
neither a predictive advantage nor the success or failure of the overall
approach. It produced an incomplete primary comparison, topology abstentions,
and prediction--observation discrepancies whose causes remain to be diagnosed.

## M8A: preservation, identity, and arithmetic

The source is M7 run `33990678586`: 160 qualified cell artifacts, four
predeclared raw `NCD/r0` audit samples, and one frozen analysis artifact. M8A
must download all 165 artifacts while they are available, record their original
GitHub metadata, hash every unpacked file, and upload the combined evidence with
90-day retention.

M8A independently checks:

1. the complete two-application by two-placement by four-law by ten-repetition
   identity matrix and the source run recorded inside each evidence boundary;
2. the hashes of all five frozen analysis tables;
3. every score row by deriving request counts and successes from the sequestered
   test-request files and evaluating the Bernoulli Brier identity directly;
4. equal-operation campaign aggregation, summary-table aggregation, and the
   equal-stratum primary proposed-minus-B2 contrast.

The temporal guard is reconstructed from successive changes in the saved test
health signals. `stable` and `stable_block_sensitivity` use the same retained
requests; only their uncertainty block lengths differ. No controller event or
test outcome enters a prediction.

Acceptance requires all 165 source artifacts, 160 unique qualified identities,
four raw samples, one analysis artifact, zero expired inputs, matching analysis
hashes, and exact counts plus numerical agreement within `1e-12` for all score,
summary, and primary-contrast checks. A failed check is evidence to locate a
specific integrity or calculation problem, not permission to discard a row.

## M8B: causal diagnostics

Only after M8A passes, M8B will decompose clean-baseline, injected-calibration,
prediction, and test rates by application, operation, failure law, and
placement. It will show full sequences and transition-relative strata, inspect
timeouts and semantic failures, enumerate successful traces with and without
the target service, and replay normalization on the four retained raw samples.
Before execution, transition distance bands are fixed at at most one second,
one to five seconds, five to fifteen seconds, and more than fifteen seconds;
requests are also separated as before or after route degradation, route
recovery, or another observed signal change. These bins are diagnostic and do
not replace the frozen primary view.

Each candidate explanation is recorded as observation, hypothesis, test,
result, and any versioned correction. `cause not established` is an admissible
result. Diagnostic examples are chosen to cover mechanisms and are not treated
as independent frequency estimates. A parser change requires a reproducible
raw-trace failure first; the other 156 cells cannot support claims about spans
that were not retained.

## Execution and interpretation boundary

Full inventory and diagnostics run only in GitHub Actions. Local execution is
limited to unit tests, configuration checks, and tiny synthetic smokes. Every
experiment or analysis job has `timeout-minutes: 360`.

M8A can reject artifact, identifier, denominator, score-formula, and aggregation
explanations. It cannot by itself explain overprediction or mixed topology
support, validate Palladio, or establish whether the article's approach is
successful. M8B remains exploratory because it follows inspection of M7.
