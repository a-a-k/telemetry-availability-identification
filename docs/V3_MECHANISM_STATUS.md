10 September 2026. This record consolidates completed evidence. It adds no hypothesis test, model fit, independent campaign or selected mechanism. Section 5 of [correction v3](SIMPAT_CORRECTION_V3.md) permits at most two mechanisms for deep testing; the selected mechanism in this study is H-EXEC-01. The remaining initial candidates have the explicit statuses below. The frozen historical graph-chain record and all failed study/engineering attempts remain unchanged.

| Candidate explanation | Evidence and status | Limit of the conclusion |
