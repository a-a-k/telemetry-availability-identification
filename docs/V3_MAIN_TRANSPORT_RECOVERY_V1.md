## Evidence and interpretation boundary

The original graph forecasts predate the original evaluator opening. Recovered PMX solving occurs later, in jobs that receive only the original calibration-derived models and controls; the original test outcomes have already been opened by the original evaluator jobs. Consequently, this repair does not restore the literal original PMX-before-first-opening chronology and cannot be presented as an unmodified successful original prospective execution. The fixed-source, fixed-data computation and isolated inputs are the recovery claim. Final reporting must name this deviation and assess its actual integrity evidence separately.

At protocol recording, the main aggregate contains no scored result. The diagnostic inspected calibration forecasts, artifact associations and evaluator qualification of three prespecified first campaigns, without using predictive errors to choose the repair. Numerical superiority, useful coverage and completed recovery are not asserted here.

Six artificial controls pass: selection beyond the first 1000 artifacts, unchanged graph-candidate preservation with digest rejection, cross-campaign PMX rejection, unchanged all/stable sufficient counts, rejection of an extra native member in a compact ZIP, and rejection of local real-data execution. The actual 240-campaign recovery remains remote. This work does not include manuscript drafting or exports.
